## Concept

- Agile is an Iterative and Incremental process and approach
    - Iterative: we repeat the process again and again
    - Incremental: We implement some features at the begining of software and then we keep on adding more features to the software
- Principle
    - Customer no need to wait for long time
    - We develop, test and release a pieces of software to the customer with few number of features
    - We can accept/accomodate requirement changes
- There will be good communication between Customer, Business Analyst, Developers and Testers
- Delivery is very fast

### Advantages

- Requirement changes are allowed in any stage of development (or) We can accomodate requirement changes in the middle of development
- Releases will be very fast
- Customer no need to wait for long time
- Good communication between team
- It is very easy model to adopt

### Disadvantage

- Less focus on design and documentation since we deliver software very faster

## Scrum

- It’s a process model
- It’s a kind of agile framework through which we will develop and test software and finally we will release the software to the customer
- It’s a framework through which we build software product by following Agile Principles
- Scrum includes group of people called as Scrum Team. Normally contains 5-9 members
    - Product Owner
        - Define the features of the product
        - Prioritize features according to market value
        - Adjust features and priority every iteration, as needed
        - Accept or reject work results
        - He always talk with the customer and get the requirements
    - Scrum Master
        - The main role is facilitating and driving the agile process
        - It’s a specific role of Scrum model
        - He makes sure people will follow agile process properly
        - He will take every step of the process
    - Dev Team
        - They will develop the software
    - QA Team
        - They will test the software
- Terminology
    - User Story: A feature or module in a software (small requirement)
    - Epic: Collection of user stories (large requirement)
    - Product backlog: Contains list of all user stories and prepared by Product Owner
    - Sprint/Iteration: Period of time to complete the user stories, decided by product owner and team, usually 2-4 weeks of time.
    - Sprint planning meeting: Meating conducts with the team to define what can be delivered in the sprint and duration
    - Sprint backlog: List of committed stories by Dev/QA for specific sprint. They will choose some of the stories to develop and test in a particular sprint.
    - Scrum meeting: Meating coonducted by Scrum Master everyday 15 mins. Called as scrumm call/standup meeting. Everybody on the team should answer the next questions
        - What did you do yesterday?
        - What will you do today?
        - Are there any impediments in your way?
    - Sprint retrospective meeting: It will conduct once after completion of every sprint. We will discuse what things go well, what things go wrong and what improvements we can implement in the next sprint. The entire team, including both the Scrum Master and Product Owner should participate.
    - Story point: Rough estimation of user stories, will be given by Dev and QA in the form of Fibonacci Series. Based on the Story Point, we will choose and planning those stories for a particular sprint.
    - Burndown chart: Shows how much work remining in the sprint. Maintained by the scrum master daily.
